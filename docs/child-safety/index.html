Child Safety Standards

ClubUp and its developer, 3MB MyClubs, have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to every ClubUp user, club, post, image, event, comment, message, and other interaction supported by the service.

1. Our commitment

ClubUp is a university community app operated by 3MB MyClubs. Although ClubUp is not directed to children under 13, child safety is everyone’s responsibility and these standards apply regardless of a user’s stated or verified age.

We prohibit use of ClubUp to create, upload, store, request, promote, distribute, facilitate, or normalize CSAE or CSAM. We act on content and conduct that violates these standards and cooperate with lawful child-safety investigations.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

ClubUp prohibits, including but not limited to:

  • Any visual depiction of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct, including real, altered, computer-generated, or illustrated material.
  • Sexualization of a child or content that depicts, praises, promotes, or jokes about the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Grooming, coercing, enticing, or soliciting a child for sexual purposes or sexual content.
  • Sextortion, threats, blackmail, or sharing intimate content involving a minor.
  • Child sex trafficking, arranging sexual contact with a child, or facilitating commercial sexual exploitation.
  • Requests for, links to, instructions for accessing, or attempts to trade or distribute CSAM.
  • Evading enforcement or using another account to continue prohibited child-safety conduct.

3. How to report a concern

In ClubUp, open the menu on a post and select Report post. Provide enough context to identify the account or content, but do not copy, download, forward, attach, or otherwise reproduce suspected CSAM.

You may also contact our child-safety team at dev3mb@gmail.com. Include “Urgent ClubUp child-safety report” in the subject and provide the relevant username, club, post, event, or other non-illegal identifying information. Do not attach suspected CSAM.

4. How we respond

When we receive or discover a credible child-safety concern, we may:

  • Review and prioritize the report according to its severity and the risk of harm.
  • Remove or restrict access to violating content.
  • Suspend or permanently terminate responsible accounts or clubs.
  • Preserve relevant account and content records when appropriate and legally permitted.
  • Report suspected CSAM or exploitation to appropriate authorities or child-safety organizations when required by law.
  • Cooperate with valid legal requests and investigations.

We do not notify an alleged offender when doing so could increase risk, compromise evidence, or interfere with an investigation.

5. Legal compliance

3MB MyClubs operates ClubUp in accordance with applicable child-safety, online-safety, privacy, evidence-preservation, and reporting laws. Where applicable law requires reporting suspected CSAM or child exploitation, we report it to the competent authority and preserve relevant information as legally required.

These standards supplement the ClubUp Privacy Policy. We may update them as the service, safety practices, or legal requirements change.
